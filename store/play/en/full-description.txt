AuraMusic turns your phone into a small broadcasting station: the sound it plays is sent live to the phones of the people right next to you. One of you starts the music, everyone hears it in their own headphones.

🎧 WHAT IT IS FOR
• Listening to the same track together, each with your own earbuds
• Sharing a playlist in the car, on the train, at a party
• Watching a video together without a speaker

⚡ HOW IT WORKS
1. Pair the phones over Bluetooth, once.
2. Whoever has the music taps “Share my sound”.
3. The others tap “Listen”. That is all.

📡 BLUETOOTH AND WI-FI AT ONCE
The sound travels over Bluetooth and, when the phones are on the same network, over Wi-Fi at the same time: the quality goes up, and if one drops, the other takes over. No Wi-Fi around? The hotspot of one of the phones is enough. No internet connection is needed.

🎚️ WHAT YOU GET
• A sync slider: closer to the original sound, or steadier
• A delay that adapts by itself when the radio link is rough
• Several phones listening at once
• Sharing and listening with the screen off
• A live spectrum visualizer
• An interface in English and French

🔒 PRIVACY
• No account, no ads, no trackers
• The sound goes straight from one phone to the other, never through a server
• The microphone is never used and nothing is recorded
• Only the phones you have paired can listen
• Open source: the code is public at https://github.com/Mafyou/AuraMusic

ℹ️ GOOD TO KNOW
• Android asks you to allow “screen recording”: that is how it lets an app capture the phone's sound. AuraMusic captures the sound only, never the picture.
• Some apps forbid capturing their sound: with those, the other phones hear nothing. AuraMusic cannot work around that choice.
• The sound arrives slightly delayed, 0.2 to 1 second depending on the link: in the same room, use headphones.
• Works between Android phones (Android 10 or later).
• Only share content you have the right to share, in a private setting.
